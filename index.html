THE LORE

Long ago, the gods ruled the world.

Kingdoms were built beneath their shadows.

Humanity worshipped them.


Then humanity forgot.

The temples fell. The statues broke. The names disappeared.


But one god remained.

A god without worshippers. A god without a name.


He waited in the darkness.

Until someone finally entered his kingdom.
