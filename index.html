THE FORGOTTEN GOD

Long ago, the gods ruled the world. Kingdoms were built beneath their shadows. Wars were fought in their names.


Then humanity forgot them.


One god remained. A god without a name. A god without worshippers.


His temples collapsed. His statues disappeared. His name vanished from every book.


But a forgotten god does not disappear.


He waits.


And when the world finally forgets him... he begins to remember humanity.
